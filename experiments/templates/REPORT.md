## Execution and results

Include metrics with numerator/denominator, aggregation and units; describe uncertainty and sample-size limitations. Link original independent human labels and adjudication. Mark synthetic examples explicitly.

## Conclusion and limitations

Answer the question using observed evidence, including negative or inconclusive outcomes. Separate quality from performance and security. Explain what cannot be concluded and why.

## Reproduction and review

Give exact configuration/commands and expected output artifacts. Record actual reproduction attempts and discrepancies. Name reviewer and link issue/PR/checks after they exist. A template or schema pass is not a successful runtime reproduction.
